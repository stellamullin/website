About me

I’m interested in the people behind the product.

Hello, I'm Stella, a recent UC Santa Barbara graduate with degrees in English, History of Public Policy and Law and Professional Writing.

Having explored Santa Barbara through journalistic eyes, I am especially passionate about entertainment, media and creative production. I held a central role in building a bridge between arts and culture and the greater UCSB student body and Santa Barbara community.

My experience includes reporting on and editing arts and culture coverage, section management, working on deadline and community engagement.
