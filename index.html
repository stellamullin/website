About me

I care about technical depth and the people using what I build.

I study computer engineering at UC Santa Barbara and work across the boundary between software abstraction and physical systems.

My interests are broad by design: full-stack products, reliability engineering, artificial intelligence, operating systems, computer architecture, and embedded development. The recurring question is how to turn a technically ambitious idea into something understandable, testable, and useful.

I have led engineering interns, automated cross-platform deployments, built AI-assisted workflows, evaluated model behavior, and developed applications from prototype through deployment.
