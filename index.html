About me

I’m interested in the people behind the story.

I’m a writer and editor at UC Santa Barbara with a focus on arts, culture, music, and local journalism.

I’m especially interested in stories that make creative communities feel tangible — whether that means profiling an artist, interviewing a musician, covering a local event, or exploring the culture surrounding a place.

My experience includes editing arts and culture coverage, writing features and profiles, interviewing artists and musicians, and reporting for publications across Santa Barbara and beyond.
